Tech

What's under the hood — the methods, and the open-source software we build on.

Methods

Embedding & mapping. A reference atlas is embedded into a batch-corrected latent space with scVI / scANVI. Your query is projected into that same space with scArches — no retraining of the reference required.

Label transfer & confidence. Cell types are assigned by k-nearest-neighbour transfer in the latent space, each with a per-cell confidence score. Cells that sit far from the reference manifold are flagged as deviant rather than forced into the nearest known type.

Downstream analysis. Leiden community detection for clustering; per-cell-type differential expression; pyDESeq2 pseudobulk for disease-state comparisons; and decoupler for pathway and transcription-factor activity.

GPU acceleration. The heavy steps run on a single GPU via RAPIDS (cuML, CuPy) and rapids-singlecell, with chunked, lazy data paths designed to scale toward atlas size.

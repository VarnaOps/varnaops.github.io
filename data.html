Data & licensing

Where our reference atlases come from, and how we handle licensing and attribution.

Reference source: CZI CELLxGENE Census

VarnaOps reference atlases are built from the CZI CELLxGENE Census — a standardized, openly available corpus of single-cell data. All data distributed through CELLxGENE is released under CC BY 4.0. That license permits commercial use; its one obligation is attribution, which we carry into every reference manifest and report.

Commercial-use filtering

Beyond the uniform CELLxGENE policy, source studies can carry their own terms — some are non-commercial (CC BY-NC) or no-derivatives (CC BY-ND), which are not usable in a paid product. Our ingestion filters datasets by license on by default, and fails loudly if a dataset's terms can't be confirmed — so non-commercial or no-derivatives data never enters a reference used in the product.

Ontology & markers

Cell-type labels are harmonized to the Cell Ontology (CL), and curated marker panels draw on CZ CellGuide — both released under CC BY 4.0, with attribution.

Per-dataset attribution

Every reference ships a manifest that lists each contributing dataset — its title, collection, DOI, and citation — so the full provenance travels with the model and appears in the report. The live human-brain reference is built from CZI CELLxGENE Census human-brain datasets.

Maintainer note: the complete per-dataset citation list should be generated from the reference manifest (reference_manifest.json / metadata.csv) rather than hand-written, and embedded here at publish time so it stays in sync with the shipped reference.

Your data

Data you upload is processed in an isolated per-run job and is not used to train our models. Your inputs and results are yours.

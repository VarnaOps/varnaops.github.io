Data privacy

How your data is handled today, and the direction we're committed to for clinical, patient, and proprietary data.

Today

The platform is built around a simple principle: your data is processed to produce your result, and nothing else. The table below reflects how the current pipeline works.

AspectHow it works today
Your dataProcessed in an isolated, per-run job. One run never shares data with another.
Model trainingYour data is never used to train or improve our models. Models are trained only on public reference atlases.
Reference atlasesBuilt solely from public, properly-licensed data (the CZI CELLxGENE Census, CC BY 4.0) — never from customer data.
In transit & at restUploads and results move over encrypted connections and are stored encrypted in cloud object storage.
Your resultsReturned to you as a self-contained, reproducible report; the run records its own provenance.
What we needThe pipeline operates on expression matrices and sample metadata — not personal identifiers.

Where we're headed

Single-cell data increasingly carries clinical, patient, and proprietary information. Our direction is to let the most sensitive data be analyzed without ever leaving the environment you control, backed by the controls regulated work requires. Concretely, we're building toward three deployment tiers:

DeploymentWhat it means
Managed cloudWe run the analysis in our cloud, isolated per customer — the simplest option for non-sensitive data.
Your cloud (VPC / BYOC)The engine runs inside your own cloud account; your data never leaves your environment, and we never see it.
Air-gapped / on-premRuns fully offline on your hardware for the most sensitive data — no external calls, with the models and dependencies bundled locally.

Alongside those, the controls we're working toward for clinical and proprietary data:

  • Access & audit — role-based access control, encryption with managed keys, and audit logs for every run.
  • Agreements & residency — data-processing agreements and configurable data residency, so data stays in the region you require.
  • Retention & deletion — explicit retention windows and on-request deletion of inputs and outputs.
  • De-identification — guidance and tooling to keep inputs de-identified, since the analysis needs expression and metadata, not identities.
  • Standards — building toward the frameworks that govern health and enterprise data (e.g. HIPAA-aligned handling, SOC 2, GDPR).
  • Ownership — your data and the results derived from it remain yours; we won't use them to improve shared models without your explicit, opt-in consent.
This page describes how the platform works today and the direction we're committed to. The forward-looking items are roadmap — capabilities we're building, not certifications we already hold. Need a specific guarantee for a pilot? Talk to us.

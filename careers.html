Careers

We're early — and always open to people who want to build this with us.

Proposals are open

We don't run fixed job listings yet. Instead, proposals are open: if you're excited about reproducible, scalable infrastructure for biology, tell us what you'd want to build or improve and how you'd contribute. A short note about you and an idea you'd run with is worth more than a formal application.

Who tends to fit

People who care about getting the fundamentals right — across infrastructure and pipelines, machine learning, single-cell and spatial biology, and the interfaces that make all of it usable. Strong opinions about reproducibility and best practices are a plus.

Get in touch

Send a proposal to leonardo@varnaops.com — what you'd work on, and why it matters.
